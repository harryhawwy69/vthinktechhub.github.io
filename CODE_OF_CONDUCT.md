# Code of Conduct

VThink Tech Hub is a learning space. Many people here are making their first contribution ever.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give feedback on the code, not the person.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Assume good intent; ask before judging.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Sharing someone's personal information without their permission.
- Spam, including low-effort pull requests made only to raise contribution counts.

## Reporting

Email ssujith@vikasinstitutionsnunna.org. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove comments, close pull requests, or block accounts that break this code.
